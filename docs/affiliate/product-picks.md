# Sparkle Finder Affiliate Product Picks

Created: 2026-05-31

Use this catalog to vet recommended products before they become app data or public links. Keep the first 30-day pilot evenly split between collector picks and rep/livestream picks.

Louis must be heavily involved in product consideration and final selection. No product should move to `ready`, `live`, or app implementation without his explicit approval.

The rows below are category-level planning ideas, not approved product selections. Exact products must be reviewed through `louis-candidate-review-packet.md` before they can move to `ready`, `live`, approved picks, app data, or public guide links.

## Approval Rules

Each approved pick must have:

- lane: `collector` or `rep`
- category
- retailer/program
- plain-English reason it fits Sparkle Finder
- disclosure notes
- link status
- placement
- product quality notes
- Louis approval
- completed Louis candidate-review packet

Do not approve picks that rely on copied retailer reviews, copied ratings, live prices, or retailer images unless the affiliate program explicitly permits that use.

Follow `trust-and-copy-guidelines.md` for every public product placement.

## Quality Gate

Before a pick is shown to Louis for approval, review:

- current rating and review count on the retailer site
- recent negative reviews and repeated complaint patterns
- seller or brand credibility
- return-policy friction or shipping red flags
- whether the product feels useful, tasteful, and on-brand
- whether the recommendation avoids unsupported claims

If a product has bad reviews, suspicious review patterns, poor availability, unclear seller quality, or cheap/junky presentation, mark it `paused` and do not recommend it.

If anyone reports a credible problem with a product or company after publication, mark the pick `paused` while it is reviewed.
