# Rep Guide Topic: Livestream Lighting Basics

Created: 2026-05-31
Lane: rep
Status: topic draft, no exact products selected

## Purpose

Help reps understand lighting categories for clearer live shows without implying that gear will improve sales, visibility algorithms, or customer conversion.

## Audience

Sparkle Suite reps who stream from home and want practical setup guidance.

## Content Outline

1. Why lighting affects viewer comfort and product visibility.
2. Common lighting problems: glare, shadows, uneven color, and cramped tables.
3. Broad lighting categories to compare.
4. Setup questions before buying.
5. How Sparkle Finder screens lighting recommendations.
