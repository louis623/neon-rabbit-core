# Rep Guide Topic: Phone Mount And Tripod Setup

Created: 2026-05-31
Lane: rep
Status: topic draft, no exact products selected

## Purpose

Help phone-streaming reps think through stability, height, angle, clamp strength, and workspace fit before exact gear is chosen.

## Audience

Sparkle Suite reps who stream from a phone and need a stable, practical setup.

## Content Outline

1. Map the live workspace before buying gear.
2. Compare height, reach, base style, and clamp style.
3. Think about phone size, case thickness, and charging access.
4. Plan for table movement and cable routing.
5. How Sparkle Finder screens mount and tripod recommendations.

## Category Lanes To Research Later

- tabletop phone tripods
- adjustable phone mounts
- overhead or arm-style mounts
- basic cable-management accessories

Do not choose exact products in this guide. Candidate products must go through the Louis review packet first.

## Trust Copy

```text
A phone mount should feel stable and boring in the best way: it holds the camera where it belongs.
```

```text
If a mount slips, wobbles, breaks, or does not fit common phones as expected, tell us so we can review the recommendation.
```

## Disclosure Notes

## Quality-Screening Prompts

- Are repeated complaints about wobble, weak clamps, broken joints, bad threading, or phone-size mismatch visible?
- Is phone compatibility clear, including large phones and cases?
- Does the product look stable enough for repeated live-show use?
- Are returns reasonable if the item is unstable in the rep's workspace?
- Does the copy avoid presenting one mount as perfect for every setup?

## Louis Approval Gate

No mount, tripod, or related accessory can move to `ready`, `live`, or app data until Louis reviews the exact candidate, quality notes, seller notes, return friction, risk notes, and final disclosure copy.
