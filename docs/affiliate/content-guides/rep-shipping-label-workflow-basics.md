## Purpose

Help reps evaluate shipping label workflow categories without implying Sparkle Suite handles fulfillment, shipping labels, carrier decisions, or operational guarantees.

## Audience

Sparkle Suite reps who want a cleaner label-printing or shipping-supply workflow.

1. Separate workflow needs from product hype.
2. Compare printer, label, and workspace categories.
3. Check compatibility before buying.
4. Understand support, returns, and replacement supplies.
5. How Sparkle Finder screens shipping workflow recommendations.

## Quality-Screening Prompts

- Are repeated complaints about setup, driver installation, label jams, label compatibility, warranty support, or return friction visible?
- Does the product state compatibility with common devices and shipping workflows?
- Are replacement supplies easy to understand and reasonably available?
- Does the seller or brand provide support that looks credible?
- Does the copy avoid promising time savings or cost savings unless Sparkle Finder has tested them?
