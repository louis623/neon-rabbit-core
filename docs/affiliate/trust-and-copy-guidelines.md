# Sparkle Finder Affiliate Trust And Copy Guidelines

Created: 2026-05-31

Affiliate products should strengthen trust in Sparkle Finder, not create quick-click clutter. Product recommendations must feel careful, credible, and useful.

This document is not legal advice. It records Sparkle Finder's working disclosure posture based on official-source summaries and should be re-checked before links go live.

## Trust Position

Sparkle Finder should be picky about affiliate products and companies.

Recommended products should be:

- useful for collectors, reps, or livestream setup
- credible enough to attach Sparkle Finder's name to
- presented honestly without hype
- easy to reconsider if customers or reps report problems

Do not recommend a product only because the commission is good.

## Copywriting Rules

Affiliate copy should say why a product is useful, not pressure people to buy it.

Use copy like:

- "A practical option for keeping collection pieces organized."
- "Worth considering if you need a simple phone-stream setup."
- "Chosen for utility, not because it is the only option."
- "If this does not work well for you, tell us so we can review the recommendation."

Avoid copy like:

- "Best ever."
- "Guaranteed."
- "Perfect for everyone."
- "Official Bomb Party gear."
- "Must-have."
- "Buy now before it is gone."

Do not claim Sparkle Finder has tested a product unless it actually has.

## Disclosure Readiness

Last official-source check: 2026-05-31

Official sources:

- Amazon Associates Operating Agreement: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement/
- Amazon Associates disclosure help: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GPXFHVYZMTGPUMPE
- FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides

Sparkle Finder rule:

- Put affiliate disclosure close to the recommendations or links it covers.
- Make disclosure visible and plain enough that a reasonable reader can notice it before clicking.
- Do not hide disclosure only in a footer, terms page, about page, or general disclosure page.
- Repeat disclosure when a page has separated recommendation areas and readers may not connect the earlier disclosure to later links.
- Keep disclosure separate from quality/trust copy.

Default general disclosure:

```text
This guide may include affiliate links, which can earn Sparkle Finder a commission at no extra cost to you.
```

Compact disclosure for cards or short placements:

```text
Paid link. Sparkle Finder may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
```

Amazon-specific disclosure:

```text
As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
```

For Amazon placements, use the Amazon-specific statement clearly and prominently in addition to any general affiliate disclosure needed near recommendations.

Do not use vague standalone labels such as:

- "affiliate"
- "partner"
- "recommended"
- "shop"
- "sponsored-ish"

## Required Product Placement Copy

Any page, guide, card, or product section that includes affiliate products should include plain trust language near the recommendations:

```text
We are picky about affiliate recommendations. If a product or company gives you trouble, please let us know so we can review the recommendation.
```

For more compact placements, use:

```text
Affiliate pick. Tell us if this product or company gives you trouble.
```

Use affiliate disclosure language separately from trust language. Disclosure explains the business relationship. Trust language explains the review posture and feedback path.

## Issue Reporting Policy

If a customer, rep, or team member reports a product/company issue:

1. Record the report in the affiliate weekly log.
2. Mark the product pick as `paused` if the issue seems credible or repeated.
3. Review the product page, recent reviews, seller/company credibility, return/support complaints, and any available program terms.
4. Decide whether to keep, replace, or remove the recommendation.
5. If appropriate, contact the company or affiliate program for clarification.
6. Record the decision in `product-picks.md`.

Potential reasons to pause or remove a product:

- repeated quality complaints
- misleading listing copy
- shipping or return problems
- poor customer support
- suspicious review patterns
- seller/company behavior that feels off-brand
- changed product quality after the recommendation went live

## Issue Report Template

Use this template in `weekly-log.md` or the relevant product notes when an issue is reported.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Date reported | YYYY-MM-DD |
| Source | Customer, rep, team member, affiliate program, retailer notice, or internal review. |
| Affected product/program | Product/listing name or category, plus affiliate program if known. |
| Complaint or concern | Plain summary of what went wrong. |
| Credibility check | First report, repeated report, verified listing change, visible review pattern, program notice, or needs more evidence. |
| Paused status | `paused`, research hold, or not paused with reason. |
| Owner | Person responsible for follow-up. |
| Action taken | Review listing, contact program/company, replace candidate, remove recommendation, monitor, or other action. |
| Resolution | Keep, replace, remove, continue pause, or reopen later. |
| Remove/replace decision | Final decision and date. |

## Customer Feedback Copy

Use this as the default feedback prompt near affiliate areas:

```text
Have a problem with one of these products or companies? Tell us right away. We review feedback and may pause, replace, or remove recommendations.
```

## Internal Standard

Sparkle Finder should rather recommend fewer products than recommend weak products.
