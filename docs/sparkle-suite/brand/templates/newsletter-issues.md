# Newsletter Issue Templates

Newsletter is a primary Sparkle Suite brand channel.

## Use this when

- you are drafting a Sparkle Suite issue for leads, interested reps, or current brand followers
- you need a reusable structure that supports both nurture and education
- you want to keep one newsletter brand while varying the angle under the hood

## Goal

- support one newsletter brand across Lead nurture and Rep education content
- help readers understand why Sparkle Suite matters in plain English
- make each issue worth opening even when it is short

## Inputs

- audience emphasis: Lead nurture or Rep education
- one main topic, feature, or workflow lesson
- one CTA matched to audience warmth

## Structure

- Subject line
- Opening tension or useful update
- Why it matters
- Main block
- Secondary support
- CTA

## What good finished output looks like

- the issue reads like one newsletter brand serving both Lead nurture and Rep education well
- the opening gives reps a clear reason to keep reading within the first few lines
- one approved feature or workflow angle stays central instead of getting buried in updates
- the CTA fits the topic and familiarity level without making the issue feel overly salesy

## Lead nurture issue

- Subject line: `What makes a live feel more polished to customers?`
- Opening tension or useful update: `Most reps are doing more behind the scenes than customers ever realize, and the setup shows up in the customer experience either way.`
- Why it matters: `Sparkle Suite is being built around the idea that a better customer experience starts with a better rep setup.`
- Main block: `Use one approved feature story, like Trade board or Live event calendar, to show how the setup can feel more organized and easier to follow.`
- Secondary support: `Add one lighter proof point or note about smoother live-show flow.`
- CTA: `Invite the reader to join the waitlist or stay tuned for more Sparkle Suite updates.`

## Rep education issue

- Subject line: `How Sparkle Suite is tightening up live-show flow`
- Opening tension or useful update: `This week we are looking at one part of the setup that helps reps spend less time patching details together.`
- Why it matters: `When the flow is smoother for the rep, the experience usually feels smoother for the customer too.`
- Main block: `Teach one approved feature angle, such as how Live queue or Email updates help reps stay more organized.`
- Secondary support: `Add a practical Nic-Nac note only when it helps explain useful support without turning the issue into AI theater.`
- CTA: `Point readers toward the waitlist, next update, or the feature angle they should watch for next.`

## Feature spotlight add-on

- Feature option: `Trade board`
- Feature option: `Live queue`
- Feature option: `Live event calendar`
- Feature option: `Email updates`
- Feature option: `SMS updates`
- Feature option: `Nic-Nac`

## Brand mistakes to avoid

- do not split the voice into separate lead and customer brands
- do not write like a hard-sell ecommerce blast
- do not overpack the issue with product jargon
- do not make Nic-Nac feel more important than the core rep-facing value story
