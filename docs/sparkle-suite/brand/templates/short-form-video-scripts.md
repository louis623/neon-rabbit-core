# Short-Form Video Scripts

Short-form video is a primary Sparkle Suite brand channel.

## Use this when

- you need a practical talking-head or B-roll script for TikTok or YouTube Shorts
- you want to explain one approved feature in a way a rep can say out loud naturally
- you want to turn a pain point into a clear waitlist or follow CTA

## Goal

- make Sparkle Suite sound real, useful, and built for reps
- show how the setup improves customer experience or live-show flow
- keep the script polished without sounding rehearsed or salesy

## Inputs

- one feature focus: Trade board, Live queue, Live event calendar, Email updates, SMS updates, or Nic-Nac
- one real rep problem
- one CTA that matches how warm the audience is

## Structure

- Hook
- Real rep friction
- Sparkle Suite angle
- Why the customer experience gets better
- CTA

## What good finished output looks like

- the script can be spoken clearly in one take without sounding stiff
- one approved feature story stays easy to follow from start to finish
- the value lands in rep language while still showing what the customer feels
- the close gives one clean next step without turning into a hard sell

## 15-second script - Trade board

- Hook: `If your trade flow still feels like a scramble, that is one of the easiest ways your live starts feeling less polished.`
- Real rep friction: `Customers can feel when the trade side gets clunky, even if the products are great.`
- Sparkle Suite angle: `Trade board is built to make that part of the show feel cleaner and easier to follow.`
- CTA: `If you want a better setup behind the scenes, join the waitlist and keep watching what Sparkle Suite is building.`

## 15-second script - Nic-Nac

- Hook: `Most reps do not need more noise. They need useful support when the live gets busy.`
- Real rep friction: `That is where behind-the-scenes tasks start piling up fast.`
- Sparkle Suite angle: `Nic-Nac is the built-in Sparkle Suite assistant, built to support the flow instead of stealing the spotlight.`
- CTA: `Follow along if you want to see how Sparkle Suite is making live shows smoother.`

## 30-second script - Live queue

- Hook: `If your show gets harder to manage the second multiple people want in, that is not just stressful for you. Customers feel it too.`
- Real rep friction: `The current patchwork approach can make a live feel slower, messier, and less confident than it should.`
- Sparkle Suite angle: `Sparkle Suite is being built to give reps a better setup, and Live queue is a big part of that smoother flow.`
- Why the customer experience gets better: `When customers can follow what is happening more easily, the whole live feels more polished.`
- CTA: `Join the waitlist if you want an edge customers can feel.`

## 30-second script - Live event calendar

- Hook: `A polished customer experience starts before the live even opens.`
- Real rep friction: `When event timing and updates live in too many places, reps do extra work and customers miss context.`
- Sparkle Suite angle: `Live event calendar helps the show setup feel more organized from the start.`
- Why the customer experience gets better: `Customers get a clearer path to follow, and reps spend less time patching details together.`
- CTA: `Keep watching if you want to see how Sparkle Suite is tightening up the whole rep setup.`

## Brand mistakes to avoid

- do not write scripts that sound like a brainstorm note or prompt output
- do not bury the feature under vague productivity language
- do not present Nic-Nac as the main product story ahead of core rep-facing value
- do not promise results the approved feature set cannot support yet
