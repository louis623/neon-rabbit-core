# Captions and CTAs

## Use this when

- you need supporting caption copy for short-form video or social posts
- you want CTA options that sound polished and helpful
- you need a fast way to keep messaging aligned with the core brand promise

## Goal

- reinforce rep advantage and customer-experience value without repeating the whole script
- keep CTAs clear, warm, and plain-English
- make Sparkle Suite feel useful, not pushy

## Inputs

- the feature or angle being highlighted
- the audience temperature: cold, warm, or ready to act
- the action you want next: join the waitlist, keep watching, or learn more

## Structure

- Caption opener
- One proof or value line
- One CTA

## What good finished output looks like

- the caption supports the main video or post instead of rewriting it
- the value line stays specific to Sparkle Suite and its approved feature set
- the CTA matches audience warmth and sounds warm, clear, and low-hype
- the overall caption feels polished enough to publish with minimal cleanup

## Caption Template 01

`A better customer experience starts with a better rep setup. Sparkle Suite is being built for reps who want smoother live shows and less patchwork behind the scenes.`

## Caption Template 02

`The difference is not just what you sell. It is how the whole experience feels to the customer from start to finish.`

## Caption Template 03

`Trade board, Live queue, and the rest of the Sparkle Suite setup are meant to help reps look more polished without adding more chaos behind the scenes.`

## CTA Options

- `Join the waitlist if you want first access to what Sparkle Suite is building.`
- `Keep watching if you want more real rep-use examples from Sparkle Suite.`
- `Learn what Sparkle Suite is building if you want a setup that feels smoother for you and your customers.`
- `For a softer CTA, use the exact phrase learn what Sparkle Suite is building.`

## Brand mistakes to avoid

- do not add fake urgency when there is no real reason
- do not use generic SaaS CTA language like optimize, maximize, or unlock synergy
- do not turn captions into a second full script
- do not oversell AI or mention unapproved features
