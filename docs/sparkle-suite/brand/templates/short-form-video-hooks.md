# Short-Form Video Hooks

Short-form video is a primary Sparkle Suite brand channel.

## Use this when

- you need a fast TikTok or YouTube Shorts opening line
- you want to frame one approved feature through rep advantage
- you want to turn a rep pain point into a waitlist or learn-more moment

## Goal

- stop the scroll with a polished, plain-English angle
- help reps picture a better customer experience
- connect one Sparkle Suite feature to smoother live shows or less patchwork

## Inputs

- one approved feature claim: Trade board, Live queue, Live event calendar, Email updates, SMS updates, or Nic-Nac
- one rep pain point or customer-experience tension
- one CTA: join the waitlist, keep watching, or learn what Sparkle Suite is building

## Structure

- Line 1: sharp tension, edge, or customer-experience angle
- Line 2: explain what feels off in the current setup
- Line 3: hint at the Sparkle Suite fix or feature story
- Line 4: land a simple CTA

## What good finished output looks like

- each hook sounds natural out loud and can open a real TikTok or YouTube Shorts clip immediately
- the language stays polished, plain-English, and rep-centered instead of sounding like marketing jargon
- the feature angle stays inside approved claims and ties back to better customer experience, smoother live shows, or less patchwork
- the CTA feels simple and useful, not loud or pushy

## Hook 01 - rep advantage

`If you want customers to feel like your live is a step above the usual, this is exactly what Sparkle Suite is being built for.`

## Hook 02 - customer experience

`A better customer experience starts with a better rep setup, and most reps are still patching that setup together by hand.`

## Hook 03 - Trade board

`Trade board is the kind of detail that helps your show feel more organized, more polished, and easier for customers to follow.`

## Hook 04 - edge customers can feel

`The reps who stand out are usually the ones with an edge customers can feel before they can even explain why.`

## Hook 05 - Live queue

`If your live starts feeling messy the second multiple people want in, Live queue is exactly the kind of smoother-show fix worth paying attention to.`

## Hook 06 - Nic-Nac usefulness

`Nic-Nac is not there to put on an AI show. It is there to give reps useful behind-the-scenes support when the live gets busy.`

## Brand mistakes to avoid

- do not sound like generic business coaching
- do not overclaim unfinished features
- do not frame Nic-Nac like a gimmick or generic chatbot
- do not drift into hype, fake-luxury language, or vague all-in-one-tool copy
