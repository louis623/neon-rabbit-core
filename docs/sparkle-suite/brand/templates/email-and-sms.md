# Email and SMS Templates

## Use this when

- you need a launch update, reminder, follow-up, or high-value message
- you want a starting point that keeps tone polished and compliant
- you need both email and SMS versions of the same message theme

## Goal

- help reps understand the message fast
- preserve trust through clarity and compliance-aware wording
- keep Sparkle Suite sounding warm and useful instead of noisy

## Inputs

- message type: launch update, reminder, follow-up, or high-value message
- the core reason the reader should care
- channel choice: email, SMS, or both
- any required compliance text

## Structure

- Subject line or opener
- Main point
- Why it matters
- CTA or reminder
- Compliance carry-forward when required

## What good finished output looks like

- the message says the important thing early and keeps the value easy to understand
- the draft sounds like Sparkle Suite: warm, polished, clear, and respectful of the reader's attention
- the email version is ready for normal send setup and footer handling without tone drift
- the SMS version keeps every mandatory compliance line intact when that language is required

## Email Template 01

- Subject line: `A smoother Sparkle Suite setup starts here`
- Opener: `We are building Sparkle Suite to help reps create a better customer experience with less patchwork behind the scenes.`
- Main point: `This update highlights one approved feature story and explains why it matters in everyday rep flow.`
- Why it matters: `Keep the value grounded in smoother live shows, better customer experience, or an edge customers can feel.`
- CTA: `Invite the reader to learn more or join the waitlist in plain English.`

## Mandatory SMS compliance lines

These lines are mandatory when the SMS draft requires compliance carry-forward.

- Mandatory line: `message frequency may vary`
- Mandatory line: `message and data rates may apply`
- Mandatory line: `consent is not a condition of purchase`
- Mandatory line: `reply STOP to unsubscribe`
- Mandatory line: `reply HELP for help`
- Mandatory line: `wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages`
- Mandatory line: `SMS opt-in data is not sold, rented, traded, or shared for third-party marketing`

## Reminder Template

- Opener: `Quick reminder from Sparkle Suite`
- Main point: `Say the useful thing early and keep the message respectful of the reader's attention.`
- Why it matters: `Reconnect the reminder to a real rep benefit instead of generic urgency.`
- CTA: `Point to the next action clearly.`

## Brand mistakes to avoid

- do not hide compliance language that needs to stay intact
- do not sound robotic or overly formal
- do not create fake scarcity or pressure
- do not write messages that could have come from any generic software tool
