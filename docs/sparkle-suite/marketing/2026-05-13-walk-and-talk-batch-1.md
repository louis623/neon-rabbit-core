# Sparkle Suite Walk-and-Talk Batch 1

**Date:** May 13, 2026
**Goal:** Film 3 simple founder-led TikTok posts in one walking session
**Rule:** Do not read like a script. Use these as talking points only.

## Before you start

- Keep each post around 20 to 40 seconds
- Record each post in short chunks
- Do 2 takes per chunk if needed
- Trim hard later
- Do not chase perfect

## Post 1 - How I got pulled into this

**Purpose:** Tell the truth about where Sparkle Suite came from

### Chunks

1. I got pulled into this because of my sister.
2. She started doing Bomb Party, and I started helping her with her website.
3. Then I built a site for Brittany too, and between the two of them I started seeing the same kinds of friction come up.
4. That's kind of where it clicked for me. I'm wired to think about workflows and pain points and how something could just work better.
5. And that's honestly where Sparkle Suite came from. Not from trying to force a business idea, just from helping real people and realizing there was something better to build here.

### Optional close

- If you're a rep, what part of your setup still feels too manual?

### On-screen text

- Start: `How I got pulled into building Sparkle Suite`
- End, optional: `Built from real rep friction`

## Post 2 - Trades slow the whole live down

**Purpose:** Start the Trade Board story with a real pain point

### Chunks

1. One of the fastest ways a live starts feeling messy is trades.
2. It's not just that they take time. They can break the flow of the whole show.
3. When that happens, customers are waiting, the momentum changes, and the live starts feeling choppier than it should.
4. That's a big reason I started building around this problem.
5. Trade Board is meant to help that part of the show feel easier to follow and less chaotic.

### Optional close

- If trades slow your live down, what part gets messy first?

### On-screen text

- Start: `Trades don't just take time`
- End, optional: `That flow matters more than people think`

## Post 3 - Why I'm keeping this rollout careful

**Purpose:** Frame the waitlist and early access honestly

### Chunks

1. I'm not trying to build a giant waitlist just to look impressive.
2. I'd rather open this carefully than overpromise and let reps down.
3. The first people through this need real attention, because I know there are going to be bugs and things to tighten up.
4. So yeah, the early access side of this is limited on purpose.
5. I'd rather build something genuinely useful than rush it and make a bunch of noise.

### Optional close

- Follow if you want honest build updates, not hype.

### On-screen text

- Start: `Why I'm keeping this rollout careful`
- End, optional: `Careful > loud`

## Walking-session checklist

- Pick a quiet route
- Keep the phone steady
- Eye line close to the lens
- Speak a little slower than feels natural
- Pause between chunks
- Refim if a chunk gets too many fillers
- Change starting spot or angle a little between posts

## Editing reminder for later

- Put clips in order
- Cut dead space hard
- Keep pace tight
- Add captions
- Use light music or no music
- Do not over-brand it
