# Sparkle Suite TikTok 30-Day Campaign

**Date:** May 12, 2026
**Owner:** Louis
**Primary channel:** TikTok
**Campaign posture:** Trust first, credibility first, careful early access, no fake urgency

## What this document is for

This is the durable working plan for Sparkle Suite's first focused TikTok campaign.

Use it to:

- keep the campaign simple and realistic
- stay consistent across sessions
- build one post at a time without needing a giant marketing system
- capture real rep pain points that can improve both messaging and product direction

## Active guardrails

- Do not interfere with active Phase 8 Sparkle Suite work in `neon-rabbit-core`.
- This campaign is documentation-first and manual-first.
- Do not promise timelines that are not real.
- Do not use hype, fake scarcity, or generic SaaS language.
- Do not turn the comments into a complaint spiral. Invite useful specifics instead.
- Do not make Nic-Nac the whole story. Keep Nic-Nac practical and secondary.

## Core campaign goal

The first 30 days are not about going viral or stuffing the waitlist.

The real goal is to:

- build trust with Bomb Party reps
- show that Sparkle Suite is being built by someone who actually pays attention
- make Trade Board feel like a real answer to a real pain point
- invite reps to talk about the friction in their jobs
- collect better market language and product insight

## Positioning spine

Sparkle Suite's TikTok should feel like:

- a founder who cares
- a builder who watches real rep workflows
- a product being shaped by actual rep pain points
- a calm, useful voice instead of a loud marketing voice

## The big public stories

### 1. Trade Board

Trade Board is the public wedge.

It is the easiest feature to understand and the easiest one to connect to:

- smoother live flow
- less trade chaos
- less waiting
- a more polished customer experience

### 2. You

You are the trust anchor.

The account should feel founder-led, not faceless software-led.

### 3. Nic-Nac

Nic-Nac is a supporting proof-builder, not the hero.

Introduce it gradually through usefulness, not hype.

## Origin story to use

Sparkle Suite did not come from trend-chasing.

It came from helping Louis's sister after she started as a Bomb Party rep in late 2025 / early 2026. Building her original site exposed how much reps still patch together by hand. Watching her and other reps up close turned one family help project into a product direction that could help more people.

Use this story in moderation. It should build trust, not become the only story the brand can tell.

## Waitlist posture

The waitlist is not meant to signal a giant launch machine.

It should be framed as:

- early access
- limited on purpose
- careful rollout
- real attention for the first reps through the system

Use language like:

- "I'd rather open this carefully than overpromise and let people down."
- "The first reps through this should get real attention."
- "Early access is limited on purpose while I tighten this up."

## Comment strategy

The comment strategy is not a vent session. It is guided field research.

The goal is to:

- help reps feel heard
- create real back-and-forth
- collect sharper language for future content
- identify product opportunities and friction points

Good comment prompts:

- "What part of your live slows things down the most?"
- "What behind-the-scenes task eats more time than it should?"
- "What part of your setup still feels too manual?"
- "What do customers seem to get confused about most often?"
- "What's one thing you wish was easier during a live?"
- "What's something non-reps never realize is hard about this job?"

## Content mix for the month

- 5 founder trust posts
- 4 Trade Board pain-point to solution posts
- 2 rep pain-point conversation posts
- 1 Nic-Nac trust-builder post

Total: 12 posts over 30 days

Recommended pace:

- 3 posts per week
- attend rep lives 3 to 5 times per week
- note recurring pain points after each live or comment thread

## 30-day schedule

### Week 1 - Why this account exists

#### Post 1 - How I got pulled into this

- Format: face-to-camera
- Focus: origin story through Louis's sister
- Goal: establish care and credibility
- CTA: "If you're a rep, what part of your setup still feels too manual?"

#### Post 2 - One thing I keep seeing in rep lives

- Format: founder commentary
- Focus: trades slowing live momentum
- Goal: start the Trade Board story
- CTA: "What part of a live tends to break the flow the most for you?"

#### Post 3 - Why I'm not trying to rush this

- Format: face-to-camera or walk and talk
- Focus: careful rollout and honest waitlist posture
- Goal: build trust and premium restraint
- CTA: "Follow if you want honest build updates, not hype."

### Week 2 - Make Trade Board the hero

#### Post 4 - Trades don't just take time

- Format: talking head
- Focus: how trades affect momentum and customer confidence
- Goal: connect the pain point to customer experience
- CTA: "If trades tend to slow your lives down, tell me what part gets messy first."

#### Post 5 - Small Trade Board preview

- Format: screen recording with Louis in the corner
- Focus: show enough to build belief without exposing internals
- Goal: give Sparkle Suite visible proof
- CTA: "Tell me if this solves the right problem."

#### Post 6 - Rep pain-point prompt

- Format: simple founder post
- Focus: invite useful specifics
- Goal: gather real language and engagement
- CTA: "What's one behind-the-scenes task that eats more time than it should?"

### Week 3 - Trust and conversation

#### Post 7 - A lot of rep tools feel disconnected from real rep life

- Format: face-to-camera
- Focus: show that Louis is paying attention to actual workflows
- Goal: build trust without sounding bitter
- CTA: "What's something you wish tool-builders understood better about rep life?"

#### Post 8 - My sister is part of why I'm building this

- Format: origin-story follow-up
- Focus: personal trust and first-hand exposure to the niche
- Goal: reinforce the human reason behind the product
- CTA: "What's one thing you wish was easier during a live?"

#### Post 9 - Customers can feel when the flow gets choppy

- Format: founder commentary or face + visual aid
- Focus: customer experience impact of trade friction
- Goal: deepen the Trade Board story
- CTA: "That kind of detail matters more than people think."

### Week 4 - Sparkle Suite starts to feel real

#### Post 10 - What I'm actually building this for

- Format: talking head
- Focus: not more tech, not AI theater, just a better setup
- Goal: clarify product philosophy
- CTA: "If you've got a rep pain point you want me to think through, drop it below."

#### Post 11 - Nic-Nac is not the main character

- Format: talking head or face + light visual support
- Focus: practical behind-the-scenes support
- Goal: build curiosity without overselling
- CTA: "I'll share more as it gets tighter, but I want it to be useful, not flashy."

#### Post 12 - What I learned this month from reps

- Format: founder recap
- Focus: summarize repeated pain points and what Louis is noticing
- Goal: show listening and product seriousness
- CTA: "Follow along or join early access if you want to stay close to what Sparkle Suite is becoming."

## The first three hooks to prioritize

1. "I got pulled into this because of my sister, and the more rep lives I watched, the more I realized this wasn't just her problem."
2. "One of the fastest ways a live starts feeling messy is trades."
3. "I'd rather open this slowly than overpromise and let reps down."

## Format guidance

Use a mix of:

- face-to-camera founder videos
- walk and talks
- screen recordings with face in the corner
- simple visual aids
- calm commentary clips

Do not overcomplicate production. The goal is consistency, not polish theater.

## CTA pool

Rotate these instead of pushing the same ask every time:

- "Follow if you want to see what Sparkle Suite is building."
- "I'm building this around real rep problems I keep seeing."
- "If you want early access when it's ready, the waitlist is there."
- "Keep watching if you want more real rep-use examples."
- "If you're a rep and this hits a real nerve, I'm listening."

Use the waitlist CTA lightly. Roughly one out of every three posts is enough.

## Live participation strategy

Attend rep lives to:

- support
- listen
- learn their language
- identify repeated pain points
- become a familiar, trustworthy presence

Do not:

- pitch aggressively in comments
- force Sparkle Suite into every interaction
- make the live about yourself
