# Sparkle Suite Public Sales Page Message Spine v1

Date: 2026-05-26

## Purpose

This is the message spine for the normal public Sparkle Suite sales page after the waitlist stage. It is not a layout and not production copy. It is the plain-English sales story that future visuals and page sections should serve.

## Core Sales Job

Sparkle Suite needs to make a Bomb Party rep feel:

- this will make my customer experience look more polished
- my live shows will be easier for customers to follow
- I can stop repeating the same details in links, posts, comments, and messages
- this is real enough and good-looking enough to be worth paying for

## Core Promise

Sparkle Suite helps Bomb Party reps give customers a polished place to follow the show, join the queue, browse trades, get updates, and stay connected.

## Hero Direction To Test

### Recommended

**Make your live-show customer experience feel more polished.**

Sparkle Suite gives your customers a beautiful place to find your live shows, follow the queue, browse trades, get updates, and stay connected, while Nic-Nac helps you keep the setup moving inside Sparkle Suite.

Primary CTA: **Get Sparkle Suite**

Secondary CTA: **See What It Does**

### Alternate 1

**Give customers a better way to follow your live shows.**

Sparkle Suite brings your show schedule, Live queue, Trade board, customer updates, and Nic-Nac support into one polished Sparkle Suite experience built for BP reps.

Primary CTA: **Get Sparkle Suite**

Secondary CTA: **See What Customers See**

### Alternate 2

**Your customers should not have to hunt for your next live.**

Give them one polished place to see what is coming up, join the queue, browse trades, and stay connected before, during, and after the show.

Primary CTA: **Get Sparkle Suite**

Secondary CTA: **See How It Helps**

## Product Proof To Show Visually

The hero visual should prove the promise with product-like screens, not generic feature cards.

Show:

- a polished Sparkle Suite customer site preview
- Live queue: who is being served and who is up next
- Trade board: wants, haves, and pending trades
- Live event calendar: next show and featured details
- Email updates and SMS updates: reminders and follow-through
- Nic-Nac: practical help inside Sparkle Suite, not the main product story

Use illustrative details like:

- `Live Thursday at 8 PM`
- `Now serving: Kayla`
- `Up next: Amanda`
- `ISO: Unicorn`
- `Reminder sent`
- `Next show saved`

These details are placeholders for visual concepting only, not real customer data.

## Section Story

### 1. First Screen

Sparkle Suite makes the rep look more professional and gives customers a clearer experience.

### 2. The Customer Problem

Customers should not have to dig through old posts, comments, DMs, and link lists to know what is happening.

### 3. The Rep Problem

Reps should not have to keep repeating the same show details, queue updates, trade notes, and reminder messages by hand.

### 4. The Sparkle Suite Answer

Sparkle Suite gives the rep a polished customer-facing site with live-show tools and customer updates that work together.

### 5. Feature Proof

- Trade board: makes trades easier to browse and request.
- Live queue: makes the line easier to follow during the show.
- Live event calendar: makes upcoming shows easier to find.
- Email updates: keeps customers informed with clearer follow-through.
- SMS updates: helps timely reminders reach customers faster.
- Nic-Nac: helps reps with setup and how-to questions inside Sparkle Suite.

### 6. Final CTA

The final section should return to the same promise:

Give customers a polished place to follow the show, and give yourself a setup that feels easier to run.

CTA: **Get Sparkle Suite**

## Copy Posture

Use:

- warm confidence
- plain English
- customer experience language
- rep advantage language
- practical automation language

Avoid:

- internal process language
- generic SaaS language
- fake luxury
- AI spectacle
- making Nic-Nac the whole story
- treating the page like an intake form

## Affiliation Disclaimer Requirement

The public sales page should clearly state that Sparkle Suite is independent and not affiliated with Bomb Party.

Recommended footer or FAQ wording:

> Sparkle Suite is an independent tool for reps. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to Bomb Party.

Recommended plain-English support line where audience clarity is needed:

> Sparkle Suite provides websites and customer-experience tools for BP reps.

Use the full `Bomb Party` name only in the disclaimer or in a lower-page clarification where necessary. Do not use it as the hero hook, page title, CTA, or visual brand signal.

## Dead-On-Arrival Words And Angles

Do not use these in the public sales page:

- `BB business`
- `launch flow`
- `post-launch`
- `backend`
- `pipeline`
- `modules`
- `SaaS workflow`
- `AI-powered platform`
- `all-in-one productivity`

Also avoid leading with price, setup mechanics, or user agreements before the visitor understands why Sparkle Suite is worth wanting.

## Current Best Bet

Start visual concepting from this line:

**Make your live-show customer experience feel more polished.**

The visual should immediately show the customer-facing site and the live-show tools around it, so the page feels like proof of what Sparkle Suite can do.
