**Reason 2 — "No current diagnosed disability":**
Directly contradicted by Mayo Clinic's 01/12/2026 clinical note, which states verbatim: *"Diagnosis confirmed for VA purposes."* Also contradicted by Mayo's "My Health Issues" record showing Anxiety on file since 11/24/2021.

**Reason 3 — "No link to military service":**
Directly contradicted by Dr. McMullan's nexus letter, which uses the exact VA legal standard ("more likely than not") and comes from a qualified physician with relevant military-medicine background.

**Reason 1 — Golden VA Clinic records deemed "not relevant" (UNVERIFIED — pending records pull):**
The April 20 session framed Golden VA Clinic as a telehealth provider that prescribed Louis anxiety medication for years (~2005-2018, while he resided in Colorado with Aurora VA as primary care). The April 21 session re-examined the Mayo 1/12/2026 note, which states: *"He has been previously prescribed sertraline while seeing an online counselor but never received an official diagnosis."* The "online counselor" is unnamed in the medical record. Whether that counselor was Golden VA telehealth OR a separate civilian provider (BetterHelp / Cerebral / Talkspace / etc.) is currently uncertain. Records retrieval (MyHealtheVet pull + C-file request via VA Form 3288) is required before this argument can be made specifically. Captured as `open_item 447c0e32`.

### Strategic Path Forward (locked April 20, 2026)

- Request informal conference with Higher-Level Reviewer (Louis can speak directly to senior reviewer; may delay decision slightly but worth it)
