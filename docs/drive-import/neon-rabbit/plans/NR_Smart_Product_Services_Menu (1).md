## "Standard Equipment" — Included in Every Build

To maintain the Neon Rabbit reputation for digital craftsmanship, every project automatically includes:

- **Neon Maintenance:** Up to 4 updates per month (capped at 2 total hours).
- **Security & Hosting:** Premium hosting, SSL certificate, and domain management.
- **Modern Visibility:** Built-in SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) readiness.

---

## The Shop "Overtime" Rate — Overage Fees

The following triggers a **$60/hr flat fee** to protect margins and manage scope:

- **Maintenance Overages:** Requests exceeding 4 per month or 2 total hours.
- **Scope Creep:** Projects exceeding 5 pages, 1,000 words per page, or 5 product uploads.
