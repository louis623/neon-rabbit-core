**🛠️ Sparkle Suite: "Wait, Why Isn't It Syncing?"**

*Keep this on your desk for a stress-free live reveal\!*

### **1\. The Website is Showing Names I Already Revealed**

* **The Cause:** You likely forgot to check the **"Revealed"** box on the Bomb Party dashboard.  
* **The Fix:** Go to your **Party Orders** page and check the box for that customer.  
* **Pro-Tip:** Once checked, you can wait for the 2-minute auto-sync or hit the pink **✨ Sync** button for an instant update.

### **2\. The Pink Sync Button is Missing**

* **The Cause:** Chrome might have "gone to sleep," or you navigated away from the Party Orders page.  
* **The Fix:** Refresh your Bomb Party **Party Orders** page. The button should reappear within 3 seconds.

### **3\. The Website "Last Synced" Time is Old**

* **The Cause:** Your computer might have slowed down, or your internet flickered.  
* **The Fix:** Click the pink **✨ Sync** button once. If it turns **Green (✅ Website Updated\!)**, you are back in business.  
* **Reminder:** The website itself only "checks" for updates every 60 seconds, so give it a moment to catch up.

### **4\. New Orders Aren't Appearing in the Queue**

* **The Cause:** New orders only appear when the dashboard page refreshes.  
* **The Fix:** Your tool is set to auto-refresh every 2 minutes. If you are in a "rush" of orders, just manually refresh your browser tab once to see them instantly.

### **5\. I Get an Error Message When Clicking Sync**

* **The Cause:** Usually a temporary glitch in the Google "Bridge."  
* **The Fix:** Refresh your dashboard and try again. If it persists, ensure you are logged into the same Google account used to set up your database.

### ---

**💡 The Golden Rules for Success**

1. **Keep the Tab Open:** Your PC must stay on the **Party Orders** dashboard for the automation to work.  
2. **Check the Box:** The "Revealed" checkbox is the "Off Switch" for each name in your queue.  
3. **Visible Window:** If possible, keep the dashboard window visible on your screen (don't minimize it) to keep the "Heartbeat" strong\!
