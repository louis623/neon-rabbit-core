# Customer Trade Workflow Decisions

Started: 2026-05-29

This file captures customer-to-customer trade workflow decisions one at a time as Louis answers them.

## Decision 1: Trading Eligibility

Decision date: 2026-05-29

Customer-to-customer trading is Plus-only for active participation.

- Free accounts can browse customer trade listings.
- Free accounts can browse the jewelry database.
- Free accounts cannot list items for customer-to-customer trade.
- Free accounts cannot initiate customer-to-customer trade proposals.
- Plus accounts can list trade-eligible items and initiate trade proposals, subject to the rest of the trade workflow rules.

Product rationale:

- Free browsing keeps the network useful and helps customers see the value of the customer side.
- Plus-only trading creates an accountability gate before customers can affect other customers.
- This keeps the first customer trade workflow simple and safer without hiding the trade board entirely.
