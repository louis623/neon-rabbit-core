# Sparkle Suite QR Flyer Option Board Design

Date: 2026-05-11

## Goal

Lock the approved branding direction for a Sparkle Suite prelaunch QR flyer option board.

This is not the finished flyer build yet.

It is the design source of truth for the chosen direction so future flyer comp work stays aligned.

## Approved direction

Chosen visual lane: `A3 / Velvet Monograph`

Chosen structure: `Promise-first`

Chosen CTA posture: `Subtle`

Chosen status framing: `Coming soon` only

## Brand intent

The flyer should feel:

- soft
- premium
- feminine
- polished
- warm
- simple
- rep-centered

This wording is internal art direction only.

It should not appear as public-facing flyer copy.

## Flyer job

The flyer should sell Sparkle Suite by leading with customer experience, then proving that promise with a small number of concrete product signals, then closing with a quiet QR waitlist action.

It should read like a premium prelaunch invitation, not a website section and not a hard-sell promo graphic.

## Locked copy hierarchy

Top line:

`Coming soon`

Headline:

`A better customer experience starts with a better rep setup.`

Support line:

`Sparkle Suite is being built for reps who want a smooth and polished customer experience.`

Proof points:

- `Trade board`
- `Live queue`
- `SMS updates`

QR CTA:

`Scan to join the waitlist.`

## Visual structure

The flyer should use a contained print composition with clear vertical hierarchy:

1. small `Coming soon` line at the top
2. large headline as the main selling statement
3. short support line beneath the headline
4. compact proof-point row
5. QR block at the bottom with a quiet CTA

The QR block should support the flyer, not dominate it.

## Visual direction details

- Use the Sparkle Suite Amethyst palette as the base system
- Keep the background soft and light, with lilac/plum influence
- Use a refined editorial frame and generous spacing
- Keep the composition calm and premium rather than busy or novelty-driven
- Preserve a flyer-like print object feel instead of stacked web-section behavior
- Let the QR area feel integrated and polished, not like a utility box pasted on top

## Tone guardrails

- Do not use vague internal descriptors as public copy
- Do not make the flyer sound like generic SaaS
- Do not drift into fake luxury or empty elegance
- Do not let the QR CTA become loud or aggressive
- Do not overload the flyer with too many features
- Do not make it look like a homepage hero or landing page section

## Copy guardrails

- Lead with customer experience, not productivity language
- Keep the support line plain English and easy to trust
- Keep the proof points truthful and limited
- Keep `Trade board`, `Live queue`, and `SMS updates` as distinct items
- Avoid feature bundling language that blurs the product story

## What this locks

This spec locks:

- the chosen visual direction
- the messaging hierarchy
- the exact current copy
- the approved proof-point set
- the quiet waitlist CTA posture

This spec does not yet lock:

- final export dimensions
- print production details
- final QR asset treatment
- final typography implementation
- final finished-flyer polish
