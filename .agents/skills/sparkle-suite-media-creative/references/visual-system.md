# Sparkle Suite Media Visual System

## Approved anchor

Louis approved `assets/approved-nic-nac-live-calendar-thumbnail.jpg` on September 4, 2026 as the basis for future Sparkle Suite thumbnails and related video media.

Treat it as a family-resemblance reference, not a requirement to clone every composition. Its reusable grammar is:

- warm blush or warm-white field
- a small outlined brand/demo pill near the top
- one very large Playfair-style headline with strong plum contrast
- a simple conceptual feature story on the opposite side
- restrained accent pink used to guide the eye
- a small complete pink S seal with its outline
- abundant negative space and no visual noise

## YouTube thumbnails

Default hierarchy:

1. One short feature, demo, or benefit headline.
2. One visual proof idea.
3. A quiet Sparkle Suite identifier.

Useful headline patterns include:

- Feature-first: `NIC-NAC + LIVE CALENDAR`
- Demo-first: `WATCH NIC-NAC WORK THE CALENDAR`
- Benefit-first: `LIVE SHOWS. LESS PATCHWORK.`

Choose wording that truthfully matches the video. Do not force these examples onto unrelated videos or use sensational claims simply to imitate creator clickbait.

Composition rules:

- Keep essential text and marks comfortably inside every edge.
- Make the headline readable at phone-thumbnail size.
- Prefer one strong illustration, real product crop, or presenter frame over a collage.
- Use the approved thumbnail as the default light treatment. A warm dark-plum treatment is allowed when the video needs stronger contrast, but it must still feel soft and premium.
- If Louis supplies a face or video frame, preserve identity and integrate it without changing the brand palette into generic influencer styling.

Delivery rules:

- Verify current YouTube thumbnail dimensions, formats, and upload limits against official YouTube Help.
- Export exact 16:9 dimensions.
- Prefer an upload-ready JPG that also fits the strictest relevant upload-size limit. Provide a lossless PNG when useful.
- Report exact pixel dimensions and byte size after export.

## TikTok and YouTube Shorts

Translate the visual system into 9:16 rather than cropping a horizontal thumbnail.

- Build the first frame and cover as a native vertical composition.
- Keep the hook in a central readable column, with breathing room for platform UI at the top, bottom, and right edge.
- Lead with one idea in the first frame.
- Use real vertical product captures, original B-roll, or a truthful abstract visual.
- Keep cover wording short; the cover should identify the story rather than carry a full script.
- Use captions and motion choices from the short-form playbooks; this reference governs the branded still/cover system.

Verify current platform export and cover behavior from official TikTok or YouTube documentation when it affects the deliverable.

## Supporting media

Use the same grammar for poster frames, title cards, end cards, overlays, or background plates:

- preserve the palette and typography relationship
- keep one focal message
- use the canonical S seal as a composited asset
- leave safe negative space for footage, captions, or controls
- avoid decorative elements that compete with the story

For full video editing, use an applicable installed video-editing workflow as well; this skill remains the visual brand and packaging layer.

## Product-screen rule

An abstract card or calendar page may communicate a concept, but it must not look like a claimed Sparkle Suite screen. Any recognizable product interface must come from a real screenshot, recording, approved demo, or explicitly reviewed mockup.

## Final review

At both full size and reduced preview size, confirm:

- exact spelling
- readable headline hierarchy
- safe crop margins
- complete pink S and pink outline
- no fabricated product state
- no generic robot or AI theater
- no purple-first, neon, glitter-heavy, gold-heavy, or dark-luxury drift
- correct platform ratio, dimensions, format, and file size
