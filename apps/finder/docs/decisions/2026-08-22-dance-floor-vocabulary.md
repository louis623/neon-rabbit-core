# Dance Floor Vocabulary

Date: 2026-08-22

## Approved Customer And Rep Language

- The product feature is the `Dance Floor`. Always capitalize it when referring to the feature.
- Jewelry offered for trade on the Dance Floor are `dancers`.
- A `trade` remains the exchange or process.
- Use generic `jewelry` when the copy is not specifically about Dance Floor inventory.

Natural examples include:

- `Add a dancer to your Dance Floor.`
- `Browse dancers.`
- `No dancers match these filters.`
- `The Dance Floor is for item-for-item swaps only.`
- `The customer requested a dancer.`

## Compatibility Boundary

Do not rename database tables, API routes, stable IDs, URL paths, field names, component names, or integration contracts solely for terminology. Compatibility names such as `/rep-boards`, `listingId`, `trade_listings`, `RepBoardListing`, and `find_rep_board_availability` may remain internally. They must not leak into customer- or rep-facing copy, accessibility labels, metadata, help text, or Nic-Nac responses.

Legacy wording may remain only in clearly marked archival research, tests that prove old input is still understood, and explicit regression-test fixtures that verify the forbidden language is caught.
