# Sparkle Finder Affiliate Command Center

Created: 2026-05-31

This folder is the source of truth for Sparkle Finder affiliate work. Use it to track program applications, approved product picks, disclosure readiness, and weekly operating notes before any affiliate link is added to the app.

## Current Status

Sparkle Finder is preparing a split 30-day affiliate pilot:

- collector essentials for customers
- livestream, tech, shipping, and setup gear for reps

No affiliate link should go live until the relevant program and product pick both have disclosure notes and a ready link status.

Louis must personally approve every product before it becomes an approved pick or live affiliate link.

The current readiness layer is live on the custom Sparkle Finder domain:

- Site URL for affiliate applications: `https://yoursparklefinder.com`
- Public disclosure URL: `https://yoursparklefinder.com/affiliate-disclosure`

It prepares content topics, disclosure copy, public trust/issue-reporting copy, and quality review gates before Sparkle Finder chooses exact products or publishes affiliate links.

## Operating Rules

- Keep the first 30 days split evenly between collector products and rep/livestream gear.
- Do not add products just because they pay well. Every pick needs a plain-English reason it helps collectors or reps.
- Do not recommend junk, weak products, products with concerning reviews, or products that feel off-brand for Sparkle Finder.
- Louis approval is required before any product moves to `ready`, `live`, or app implementation.
- Review affiliate terms monthly, especially Amazon rules.
- Add disclosure requirements before links go live.
- Avoid live prices, product ratings, copied reviews, or retailer images unless the program explicitly allows that use.
- Keep Sparkle Finder positioned as a discovery hub, not a jewelry marketplace.
- Do not imply Sparkle Finder or Sparkle Suite is officially affiliated with Bomb Party.
- Affiliate copy must reinforce quality, credibility, and trust. Use `trust-and-copy-guidelines.md` for wording and issue-reporting rules.
- If anyone reports a problem with a product or company, record it, review it, and pause the recommendation when appropriate.
- Do not select exact products from guide topics alone. Every exact product must pass the Louis candidate-review packet first.
- Public disclosure should appear close to affiliate recommendations and should not rely only on a footer or general disclosure page.
- Amazon placements must include the required Amazon Associate statement before Amazon links go live.

## Weekly Rhythm

- Monday: update `program-tracker.md` statuses and choose the week's top 3 actions.
- Midweek: add or revise product picks in `product-picks.md`.
- Friday: update `weekly-log.md` with what changed, what worked, and what needs attention.
- Before app implementation: convert only ready product picks into app data.

## Readiness Checklist

Before any affiliate link goes into Sparkle Finder:

- [ ] Program is `approved` or explicitly documented as `otherwise eligible` in `program-tracker.md`.
- [ ] Terms URL is recorded.
- [ ] Disclosure requirement is recorded.
- [ ] Product pick has a collector or rep lane.
- [ ] Product pick has a clear reason it fits Sparkle Finder.
- [ ] Product quality has been reviewed for ratings, review patterns, seller/brand credibility, return friction, and fit.
- [ ] Product pick has no unresolved risk notes.
- [ ] Louis has approved the exact product/listing. Category approval is planning-only and does not satisfy `ready`, `live`, or app-data readiness.
- [ ] Affiliate placement copy includes trust language and a clear path for reporting product/company issues.
- [ ] Link status is marked `ready`.
- [ ] Placement is recorded.
- [ ] The relevant content guide has no exact product names, prices, ratings, review snippets, or retailer images unless later permitted by terms.
- [ ] The exact product has a completed Louis candidate-review packet.
- [ ] Louis has explicitly approved the exact product, retailer/program, placement, disclosure copy, and trust/issue copy.
