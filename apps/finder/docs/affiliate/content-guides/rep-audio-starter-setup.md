# Rep Guide Topic: Audio Starter Setup

Created: 2026-05-31
Lane: rep
Status: topic draft, no exact products selected

## Purpose

Help reps compare simple audio setup categories so customers can hear lives more clearly, while avoiding unsupported technical claims.

## Audience

Sparkle Suite reps who stream from a phone, tablet, or computer and want a beginner-friendly audio path.

## Content Outline

1. Why clearer audio matters during a live.
2. Basic setup questions: device, room noise, distance, and movement.
3. Broad audio categories to compare.
4. Compatibility and return considerations.
5. How Sparkle Finder screens audio recommendations.

## Category Lanes To Research Later

## Quality-Screening Prompts

- Are recent complaints about connection failures, device incompatibility, battery life, background noise, or poor support repeated?
- Does the product clearly state which devices and ports it supports?
- Is setup simple enough for a starter guide?
- Is return friction low if the product does not work with a rep's device?
- Does the copy avoid claiming studio quality unless Sparkle Finder has tested that result?
